Rounded ends — the thing you actually meant

⚠ Two different features get called “rounded”, and the sections above chase the wrong one:

rounded CORNERS — where strokes MEET are filleted  →  Rubik, Red Hat, Figtree rounded TERMINALS — the stroke ENDS are semicircular  →  what you want

Faces with genuinely round stroke ends, and whether they ship a drawn italic:

Nunito — italic ✓ M PLUS Rounded 1c — no italic Varela Round — no italic Baloo 2 — no italic Quicksand — no italic Comfortaa — no italic Fredoka — no italic

⭐ Nunito is essentially the only one that is both. Round-terminal faces are usually display families, and display families skip italics.

⭐ Or round ANY face yourself

Since the mark is being outlined to paths anyway, the terminals can be rounded MECHANICALLY: stroke the glyphs in their own fill colour with stroke-linecap="round" and stroke-linejoin="round", under paint-order="stroke fill". The stroke swells the shape outward and every end and corner comes back round. ⚠ SVG only — CSS -webkit-text-stroke exposes no linecap, which is why this is demonstrated in real SVG below.

  • ⭐ It removes the italic constraint entirely. Any face with a drawn italic can be given round ends, so the choice stops being “which round face has an italic” and becomes “which letterform do I want”.
  • ⚠ It rounds EVERYTHING uniformly — corners you might want crisp go too, and the letter gets heavier by the stroke width. Drop the weight axis to compensate: a 700 with a round cap lands near an 800 without one.
  • ⚠ Counters tighten. The stroke grows inward as well as outward, so the D bowls close first. Check them before the outer silhouette.

The squeeze nobody asked for

Every mark in index.html carries textLength="146" lengthAdjust="spacingAndGlyphs", which stretches or compresses the actual glyphs to force both lines to the same width. It is an intentional device — it aligns the wordmark and the tagline into one block — and all five themes use it.

⚠ But it bites hardest on narrow faces. Georgia and a rounded sans are both narrower than Arial Black, so they get pushed further. Judge whichever face you pick at its squeezed width, not its natural one — or bake the width into outlined paths and drop the attribute for this mark.

What I'd pick, and why

  • Nunito ExtraBold. Rounded enough to stop fighting the theme's 16 px tab radius and its slow unfold, humanist enough not to read as a toy. It also has real weights, so the tagline can sit at a lighter one instead of being faked.
  • Sap amber over the stale olive. #A89000 predates the tree retheme — it is a desaturated yellow-green sitting on a desaturated green band, which is why it looks muddy rather than wrong. This is the same miss the retheme already warned about for --edit-glow-rgb; the glow was caught and the logo was not.
  • ⚠ The italic requirement narrows the field. Every shipping mark is italic, and only Nunito and Asap ship a drawn italic — Baloo 2, Varela Round and Quicksand get a browser shear instead. For a mark you will outline anyway that is survivable, but a sheared letterform is a worse starting point to trace than a drawn one.
  • Outline it to paths. A logo whose shape depends on which fonts a viewer has installed is not a logo — and outlining lets the 146-unit width be baked in rather than applied as a distortion.
  • Organic only. The other four marks sit on bands that never moved. The squeeze is shared, so if you change that, say so rather than silently altering all five.
